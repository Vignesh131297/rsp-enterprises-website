ABOUT US · SINCE 2000

25 years of moving earth for India's builders

Excavator loading a tipper during basement excavation

A full-service engineering & construction firm

RSP Enterprises is a full-service engineering and construction firm with 25 years of experience, specialising in excavation.

Operating across Bangalore and Hosur, we deliver basement and bulk excavation, shoring, hydraulic rock breaking, controlled blasting, demolition, debris clearing and building & land development for corporate campuses, residential towers and private clients.

With a highly skilled team and our own fleet of 22 machines and vehicles, we deliver precise solutions tailored to each site and manage complex projects seamlessly, from the first dig to the finished building. Our water tankers and on-call ambulances keep every site supplied and supported, so clients work with one dependable partner from start to finish.

Quality is never compromised – every project meets the standard our clients trust.
